# DataGenie Public Site — Design Direction

## Three distinct approaches

### 1. Trust Fabric
**Very Brief Intro:** A precise, editorial enterprise aesthetic that treats governance as an operating system for trustworthy data. Deep ink fields, warm paper surfaces, and structured evidence motifs make the product feel accountable rather than promotional.

**Probability:** 0.037

### 2. Observatory Grid
**Very Brief Intro:** A technical systems-atlas direction built around luminous maps, node fields, and analytical diagrams. It emphasizes discovery and lineage as an explorable knowledge network.

**Probability:** 0.064

### 3. Steward's Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A confident, document-inspired visual language that feels like a modern governance journal. It uses dense typographic rhythm, stamped statuses, and calm archival color.

**Probability:** 0.081

## Chosen approach: Trust Fabric

### Design Movement
The site adopts **editorial enterprise modernism**: a blend of Swiss information design, premium B2B product storytelling, and operations-room clarity. It will feel like a well-designed control plane rather than a generic SaaS landing page.

### Core Principles
1. **Trust is visible:** Every section uses evidence, ownership, quality, and review language instead of vague automation claims.
2. **Asymmetric clarity:** The layout uses editorial side rails, offset cards, and broad horizontal modules rather than repetitive centered boxes.
3. **Operational narrative:** The page tells the journey from discover → understand → govern → prove, always retaining the human stewardship boundary.
4. **Deliberate restraint:** Color carries meaning, data labels are concise, and motion is purposeful rather than decorative.

### Color Philosophy
Deep midnight navy communicates control and high-stakes operational confidence. Bone-white surfaces make technical documentation highly legible, while electric cobalt signals the DataGenie system layer. Jade represents healthy evidence, amber represents review, and coral is reserved for unresolved risk. The signature color is **Signal Cobalt (#5468FF)**: a crisp, ownable blue for governed action and connected intelligence.

### Layout Paradigm
The website uses a **signal rail**: a fixed visual rhythm of slim left-side indices, large editorial content columns, and alternating wide/narrow evidence modules. Hero content is left-aligned and paired with an interactive-looking product tableau, not centrally stacked. Documentation uses anchored topic sections and collapsible process narratives.

### Signature Elements
1. A fine-grain data lattice behind hero and section transitions.
2. Evidence chips and proof lines that pair a state with its basis, such as “Fresh · explainable · owner assigned.”
3. Cobalt constellation paths that connect catalog, quality, lineage, and stewardship concepts.

### Interaction Philosophy
Navigation should make complex governance concepts feel approachable: section jumps, clear topical chips, progressive disclosure, and buttons that guide a reader to a defined outcome. Calls to action use measured language such as “Explore the control plane” and “Read the governance model.”

### Animation
Use a 180–260ms custom ease-out for hover and navigation states. Hero constellation paths may slowly drift with opacity-only/transform-only motion, while evidence cards rise a few pixels on hover. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no motion is required to understand the site.

### Typography System
**Manrope** provides the product-forward display hierarchy with tight, confident headings. **IBM Plex Sans** provides readable body copy and documentation. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for request IDs, policy rules, endpoint examples, and source/evidence labels. Headings are large, left aligned, and weight-contrasted; body copy is compact but comfortably spaced.

### Brand Essence
**DataGenie is the governed intelligence layer that turns enterprise data into trusted, explainable decisions.**

Personality: **assured, exacting, collaborative**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines make a precise claim and then establish the operating principle. CTAs describe the next learning or operating step, never a generic conversion action.

Example lines: “Make every data decision traceable.” and “Give stewards evidence, not another inbox.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The provided DataGenie logo is the primary visual mark and will appear prominently in the global header and product UI. The site will pair it with a compact generated constellation glyph as a supporting favicon/section marker, never as a replacement for the supplied brand mark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Cobalt — #5468FF**.

## Style Decisions

- The signal rail is mandatory across every major section, using the constellation mark, an index line, and compact mono labeling to make the site feel like one governed operating manual.
- Every abstract visual is paired with a concrete governance artifact such as evidence context, status chips, proposal boundaries, ownership, impact, or audit logic.
- Semantic color is reserved for meaning: Signal Cobalt for system action and connection, jade for healthy evidence, amber for review, and coral for unresolved risk or a fail-closed result.
